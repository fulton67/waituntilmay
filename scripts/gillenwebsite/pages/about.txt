ABOUT

DYLAN NEUSTADTER is a multi-instrumentalist, producer, and mixer who came up as Rick Rubin's head engineer. During his time with Rick, he worked across everything from Harry Styles, The Strokes, The Red Hot Chili Peppers, Kendrick Lamar, etc. and since leaving Rick to focus on his own work, he's recently worked with SZA, Stormzy, Sevdaliza, Teezo Touchdown, Obongjayar, Freak Slug, The Gossip, Paris Texas, Kwes Darko, Emile Haynie, Orion Sun, Arlo Parks, and Jack Penate.
