About the Red Sea, the Red Sea is an important sea route that is located between Africa and Asia. It is an intercontinental entrance gate of the Indian Ocean. This sea is connected to the Arabian Sea and the Indian Ocean through the Adan Khadi and Baab Al Mandeb Water Bridge.
The northern part of it is the Sinai Plateau, which is located between the Aqaba Plateau and the Suez Plateau. From here, it is connected to the Bhumadhya Sea through the famous Suez Canal. The Red Sea is famous for its hot and salty water and it works as a major sea route between Europe and Asia. The color of its water keeps changing due to the proliferation of Sehwal, which gives it a unique form.
From the scientific point of view, this ocean is located between the Arab and North African tectonic plates in a dense region. Many countries from the Red Sea, such as Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Sudan, Eritrea, and Djibouti, have borders.
