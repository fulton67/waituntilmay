# Lifting without surgery: threads, Morpheus8, EMFACE

Everyone in this theme is asking one question: can you get a lift without a facelift? Clinics answer yes with thread-lift before-and-afters, the "foxy eye", and EMFACE sold as a needle-free facelift in 20 minutes. Patients film Morpheus8 week by week, from bruising to a "snatched" jaw, or use it on loose belly skin after pregnancy or a 130 lb weight loss. Then come the regret videos: Morpheus8 that melted facial fat, thousands spent for nothing. Even a doctor demonstrating EMFACE says it is not a facelift. People watch to find out whether it works, whether it hurts, and whether they will be the one it backfires on.
