# Hair loss and hair "botox"

Two different conversations share this theme. One is salon "hair botox", a conditioning treatment for frizz with no toxin in it, shown off by influencers and salons in Tagalog, Portuguese and English with before-and-after flips; it gets the biggest numbers. The other is medical hair loss: dermatologists warning that minoxidil makes you shed before it works and takes months to judge, that you need a diagnosis before a treatment, that plucking grey hairs can kill the follicle, and that topical minoxidil can kill your cat. A third strand is the derm-founder story, one doctor launching her own scalp serum. People come for a verdict on what actually regrows hair.

## The numbers

28 videos, 3% of the niche. 18% of them are winners and the median one does 1.32× its creator's usual, rising to 1.79× for the ones he could make; only 46% are videos Dr. Ostad could make himself, because most of the rest is salon hair botox and haircare brand launches.

## What people claim

- Plucking grey hairs doesn't make more grow, but plucking the same one again and again kills the follicle and leaves thin patches, so dye or trim instead (63×).
- Minoxidil causes shedding in weeks 2 to 8, takes 4 to 6 months to judge, and only works while you keep using it (20×).
- Constantly pulling hair back tightly causes traction alopecia, and over time that loss can become permanent (12×).
- Ranked by a dermatologist: minoxidil serum 10, minoxidil tablet 9, PRP 8, exosomes 7, biotin 3, onion juice 1.
- Treat the diagnosis, not the symptom: pattern hair loss needs minoxidil plus hormonal medicine, while telogen effluvium needs its trigger found.
- Microneedling the scalp once a week with a stamping device, on top of daily minoxidil, boosts growth.
- Topical minoxidil on hands and pillowcases can poison and kill cats, so wash hands, dry hair before bed, or ask about the oral form.
- Recurring coarse chin hair in women is often hormonal; laser works for dark hair, electrolysis for light hair.
- A minoxidil-free, 16-active scalp serum regrew one dermatologist's hair "from this to this in three months" (her own brand, 6.3×).
- Salon hair botox fills damaged cuticles to cut frizz and add shine, and you can do it at home (the top two videos, 545× and 307×).

## How it sounds

Doctors are 61% of the speakers, but the two biggest hits are influencers doing hair botox, and the tone leans salesy (32%) and chatty (29%) more than clinical (25%); a quarter of the theme is outright ads. The doctor videos that win open with a rule or a scoreboard rather than a lecture: the top doctor clip starts, in Arabic, by telling women never to pluck their grey hairs, and the rating video opens straight into the scores. The founder videos open on a confession or a secret.

## What the audience says back

Almost nothing is recorded for this theme: only one video has comment data, and none of the 47 viewer questions from run 1 is about hair. Under a Tagalog hair-botox plug, viewers said their hair felt sticky the next day and asked whether it works on coloured or rebonded hair, where to buy it and how much it costs. The medical claims point to what people are worried about without saying it: that early shedding means the treatment is failing, that the loss will be permanent, and that nothing without minoxidil in it really works.
