## 2. Hook detail candidates (ranked)

1. **In a randomized trial, CO2 laser resurfacing cleared most precancers (83–92% across the three treatments tested) and was followed by fewer new skin cancers than in untreated controls.** Hantash 2006. [PMID 16924046](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16924046/). Small trial, so say “a small trial”. It is the payoff only a Mohs surgeon can deliver.
2. **Skin-cancer patients treated with non-ablative fractional laser had about half the rate of new facial skin cancers (21% vs 40%).** Benson 2023. [PMID 36728065](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36728065/). Retrospective, 43 vs 52 patients; it applies to Fraxel Dual-type lasers, not CO2.
3. **Healing after resurfacing takes 3 to 10 days.** ASDS. Useful framing but not surprising.

**Chosen: #1** (spoken carefully as “a small randomized trial”).

## 4. Do not say on camera

- “Laser prevents skin cancer.” The data are one small RCT and one retrospective cohort (#6).
- “No downtime” or “painless.”
- Any FDA claim for CO2 and cancer prevention. Non-ablative fractional lasers are FDA-cleared for actinic damage (noted in Benson 2023); that is not a cancer-prevention indication.
- Downplaying warning signs. Herpes reactivation can scar, so tell viewers to call rather than wait.
