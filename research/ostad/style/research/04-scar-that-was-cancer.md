## 2. Hook detail candidates (ranked)

1. **What you can see is not the whole tumour. Surgeons needed about 4 mm of normal-looking skin beyond the visible edge to clear 95% of small basal cells.** Wolf & Zitelli 1987. [PMID 3813602](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3813602/). It sets up the Mohs-map reveal (“this is what was under it”).
2. **On darker skin, about half of basal cells are brown, so they can pass for a mole.** SCF. A twist that broadens the audience, but it partly cuts against the video's “not like a mole” line; use it as a one-line aside.
3. **5.4 million keratinocyte cancers in the US in one year.** Rogers 2015. [PMID 25928283](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25928283/). A big number, but a familiar one.

**Chosen: #1.**

## 4. Do not say on camera

- “Basal cell never spreads” or “it's the harmless one.” Metastasis is rare but reported, and local destruction can be serious.
- A fixed deadline (“exactly 4 weeks”) presented as a rule. The source says weeks, or heals and returns (#7).
- Implying every scar-like spot is cancer.
- Identifying details beyond what the patient consented to (face, name, location). She needs written consent for social media specifically.
