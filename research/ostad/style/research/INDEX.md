## Changes that matter most before filming

1. **10: “Every treatment sheds before it grows” is wrong.** Only minoxidil has a labelled temporary shed (up to 2 weeks). The scorecard also omits finasteride, which beat topical minoxidil at 48 weeks in a network meta-analysis, and “minoxidil beats PRP” is not what the head-to-head trials show (no significant difference in density). Exosomes, which he sells, have no FDA-approved products.
2. **06: the 99% cure rate needs its qualifier.** Say first-time BCC, five years. The randomized trial gives about 96% at ten years for high-risk primary facial BCC, and the Mohs vs excision difference for primary BCC was not statistically significant.
3. **07: “daily mineral sunscreen” should be “tinted mineral sunscreen”** (iron oxides block visible light). Never present LUMA Cream as FDA-approved or related to Tri-Luma, the only FDA-approved melasma cream (8-week label).
4. **03: the Monk scale is his practice choice, not a validated standard** for choosing peels or lasers.
5. **08: Jeuveau is FDA-approved only for frown lines.** Don't film Jeuveau going into the forehead while implying it's approved there. Brow drop occurs in 2% even in trials, so avoid “a good injector never causes this”.
6. **09: call it “GLP-1 medicines like Ozempic and Wegovy”.** Ozempic's approval is for type 2 diabetes. Sculptra is not approved for temples.
7. **02: Botox Cosmetic is approved for moderate-to-severe lines.** Treating a 24-year-old with no lines is outside the label; keep it as “a choice, not a need”.

## Method and limits

- Sources were pulled via Europe PMC (PubMed IDs and abstracts), FDA labels and notices (fda.gov, accessdata.fda.gov, DailyMed, and AbbVie's FDA-approved BOTOX Cosmetic PI), and the Skin Cancer Foundation and ASDS patient pages. No blogs or news sites were used as the source of a fact.
- Where only an abstract was read, the claim was checked against the abstract's numbers. Full texts were not available for the peel review and several labels, so some wording (e.g., AUC scenario specifics in 06) rests on the abstract plus established guideline content; verify before quoting exact figures on screen.
- Coverage numbers come from `research/ostad/data/videos.json` (outlier_score, views), matched by keyword on title, caption, opening line, claim, on-screen hook and subject. Small or non-English videos may be missed.
- Lines that depend on his own practice (“about once a month”, “two years of PRP results”, “faces that started at 24”) cannot be verified here; use them only if true.
