**One-line argument:** "Filler fatigue" (stretched, overfilled, migrating filler) isn't real. Those results come from improper injections, untrained injectors and too much filler. The real problem is aging: volume, fat and support are lost and the skin sags. His answer is filler on the cheekbones by microcannula, which lifts because the supporting ligaments weaken with age. Migration only happens with the wrong filler or too much in a lip, and he avoids golf-ball filling of the mid cheek. Fillers are here to stay.
