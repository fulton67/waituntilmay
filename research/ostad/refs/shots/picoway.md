# PicoWay

**What it does:** Picosecond pulses shatter pigment by pressure (photoacoustic) rather than heat.  
**Anatomy:** epidermis, dermis, melanin, tattoo ink  
**Runtime:** 13 s across 4 shots · 2 overlay, 2 full-frame

OVERLAY = rendered with alpha (PNG sequence + WebM VP9 alpha), floats beside Dr Ostad on the filmed plate. FULL-FRAME = camera inside tissue or at cell scale; cut away from him for these.
