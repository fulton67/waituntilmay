# Forehead lines toxin

**What it does:** Small doses in the frontalis soften horizontal lines; too much or too low drops the brow.  
**Anatomy:** frontalis, brow  
**Runtime:** 20 s across 5 shots · 4 overlay, 1 full-frame

OVERLAY = rendered with alpha (PNG sequence + WebM VP9 alpha), floats beside Dr Ostad on the filmed plate. FULL-FRAME = camera inside tissue or at cell scale; cut away from him for these.
