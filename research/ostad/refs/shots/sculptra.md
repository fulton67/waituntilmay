# Sculptra

**What it does:** Poly-L-lactic acid particles that trigger the body to make its own collagen over months.  
**Anatomy:** deep dermis, subcutaneous fat, temples  
**Runtime:** 12 s across 3 shots · 2 overlay, 1 full-frame

OVERLAY = rendered with alpha (PNG sequence + WebM VP9 alpha), floats beside Dr Ostad on the filmed plate. FULL-FRAME = camera inside tissue or at cell scale; cut away from him for these.
