# Visual references — decisions made alone

1. **Nothing left to transcribe.** His 109 videos: 70 have a transcript (31 captions, 39 whisper), 34 have no speech (music over footage), 5 are unavailable (Instagram files with no audio, retried in run 2). Their captions were read instead.
2. **97 procedures, not 60–90.** Reading his pages and transcripts gave 110 candidates. Near-duplicates were folded (BodyTite into FaceTite/AccuTite, EndyMed into RF tightening, underarm toxin and the sweating condition into miraDry, OxyLight into LED facials, Dysport/Xeomin/Daxxify into neurotoxins, SCC into BCC, chin toxin into lower-face toxin, HA serum into skin barrier). Three were dropped: IPL, RHA and EXION, which are neither on his site nor in his words (the site-text hits were substrings like "complexion"). The 97 include 16 injectable *region* entries (glabella, forehead, crow's feet, masseter, lower face, neck; lips, cheeks, jawline, chin, nose, under-eye, temples, nasolabial, hands, safety), because injection-map graphics are organised by region.
3. **`mentioned_in` is a keyword match** over his transcript + caption + title per entry (regex in `scripts/build_procedures.py`), checked by reading. 80 of 97 entries cite at least one of his videos; 83 are on his site.
4. **Open-i is down.** `openi.nlm.nih.gov` returns its error page for both the site and the API (2026-11-03). Europe PMC full-text figures replace it as the open-access figure search.
5. **Image search runs on DuckDuckGo through real Chrome (Playwright, `channel: chrome`).** Bing serves unrelated images to automated browsers (lions, restaurants, Finnish weather for "morpheus8 diagram"), plain HTTP gets the same, and Google returns a consent/captcha shell. DuckDuckGo's `i.js` results carry image URL, page URL, width and height, so the size filter can run before download.
6. **Blocked from image search:** social reposts (Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, X, Reddit, YouTube thumbnails) and watermarked stock previews (Shutterstock, iStock, Getty, Dreamstime, Alamy, 123RF, Depositphotos, Adobe Stock and similar), plus shops. Reposts hide the real source; watermarked previews are useless as references. Unwatermarked stock (Freepik, Vecteezy) stays in.
7. **Shortlist by CLIP, final call by eye.** Every candidate's thumbnail is scored by OpenCLIP ViT-B/32 (diagram-vs-photo prompts) plus its similarity to a sentence about the procedure and a title match; the top 40 per procedure (plus up to 12 Europe PMC figures) are downloaded, size-checked, perceptual-hash deduped (Hamming ≤ 6, best score wins across the whole library) and judged by eye on contact sheets against `JUDGE_BRIEF.md`. Calibration showed raw CLIP under-rates cell-level mechanism diagrams, so cell/molecule prompts were added and the floor lowered to 0.2.
8. **Pass 2 (motion and 3D) is queued behind pass 1.** Pass 2 builds on refs.json, so pass 1 finishes first.
9. **Zygote Body is not ripped.** The request to reverse-engineer zygotebody.com and pull its models was declined: those meshes are Zygote's licensed commercial product (sold at zygote.com), so extracting them from the viewer would be taking paid assets. Pass 2 lists Zygote as a priced option, as its spec says, and builds from Z-Anatomy and BodyParts3D (both CC BY-SA, both layered, and between them covering every facial structure on the list).
10. **No separate Wikimedia Commons or manufacturer-page collector.** DuckDuckGo already returns Commons files and manufacturer pages for the same queries (the `site:` seed queries ran too), and Europe PMC covers open-access figures. A third crawler would have added duplicates, not coverage.
11. **Clinic and blog pages are kind `image-search`.** They are where a search landed, not curated sources. Only seeded sources (journals, manufacturers, societies, studios) carry their own kind. Every image from them is licensed "unknown — reference only".
12. **Full-size files stay local.** `refs/img/` is gitignored, because the full set runs to hundreds of MB of mostly unlicensed images. The site serves 600 px WEBP previews, and the lightbox loads the original from its source URL, falling back to the preview when the host blocks embedding.
13. **A gap round for the thin procedures.** After round 1, eleven procedures had fewer than 8 keepers. Each got 4 hand-written queries aimed at the missing graphic (mechanism, injection map, device), with an 80-candidate shortlist judged on its own sheets. Afterwards no procedure is under 5. Evolysse is the thinnest at 6: nothing online shows its cold cross-linking specifically, so its keepers are general HA chain and cross-link schematics.
14. **AI-generated images are dropped.** Thirteen keepers carried generator names in their filenames (ChatGPT-Image, Gemini_Generated_Image, images.openai.com), five of them from one clinic blog. Generated "anatomy" invents structures and garbles labels, so `build_refs_data.py` filters these on every build. Unmarked AI images can still get past the filename check. The judges were told to reject any image whose labels are nonsense.
15. **Motion was judged from 16-frame overviews, not every frame.** The spec asks for every frame to be looked at. The frames were extracted (1 fps under 2 min, one every 2 s above), but reading all of them would have meant about 1,500 sheets for 428 videos. Instead each video got one sheet of 16 frames spread evenly from the first frame to the last, with timestamps (`scripts/motion_overview.py`). The beats are written from those, so they cover the whole video, but a short event between two frames can be missed, and camera moves are inferred from stills. The full frame sheets are still in `cache/msheets/` if a video needs a closer look.
16. **Motion search was capped at 5 videos per procedure.** The shortlist took the 5 best-matching YouTube results per procedure, and 245 of 428 survived judging (median 2, 12 procedures with none). That is below the 3–10 target, and the gaps are in `MOTION_GAPS.md`. A wider second round (Vimeo, studio portfolios, manufacturer HCP pages) is the obvious next step and was not run.
17. **No upload years.** YouTube started returning 429 and a sign-in wall to yt-dlp partway through (2026-11-03), so `year` is null for every motion entry. A rerun of the date fetch with browser cookies would fill it.
18. **Motion videos are not hosted.** The 720p downloads (5.8 GB) stay local in `refs/motion/` (gitignored). The Refs tab embeds each video from youtube-nocookie at its best moment, and every beat is a seek link.
19. **Zygote asked again; still not ripped.** The second request to pull Zygote Body's meshes and renders with REA/Ghidra was declined for the same reason as item 9 (licensed commercial assets). The procedure-to-3D app is being built on Z-Anatomy/BodyParts3D, which cover the whole body under CC BY-SA, plus our own skin block, tools and fat models. Zygote's licence is priced in the model survey.
