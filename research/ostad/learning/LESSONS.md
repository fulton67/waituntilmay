## Reels (2026-11-17, Oct 14 injection reel v2 → v3; append-only, general for future reels)

| # | Lesson | Check |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Never put text on a flat filled box ("THE SOURCE / Deflation" on a solid block was banned). A label is type plus pen, or glass with real depth. | `lint_compositions.py` rules BOX1 / BOX2 (fixtures `bad_textbox.tsx`, `clean_glass_label.tsx`; `--selftest`). |

## Under-eye filler reel (2026-11-17, v1 → v3; see research/ostad/style/DECISIONS.md 15–15d)

| # | Lesson | Check |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | Cut only in measured silence. A recogniser's word times are off by up to 0.25 s; a cut on them chops a phoneme ("We" heard as "e"). Snap every in/out into a gap ≥ 80 ms on the audio envelope, keep ≥ 25 ms of it on the speech side, fade 15 ms. | `undereye_prep_v2.py check` refuses a piece without a gap; `verify_v2.json cuts.min_gap_ms` ≥ 80; `undereye_prep_v2.py listen` re-transcribes every piece alone: first and last words whole. |
| U2 | Remove a name by cutting the whole phrase around it, or a different line, never by clipping between two words. Redact it on read, by spelling and by the catalog's "[name]" context (the recogniser spells it differently each time). | `verify_v2.json name.name_like_words_in_render_audio` = 0. |
| U3 | Hook on information and picture, not chronology: the real result first, the patient's own setup-and-"but" over it. | `verify_v2.json hook`: a picture change or a text line ≤ 1.5 s. |
| U4 | Never split a setup from its "but", and re-read the cut transcript alone: a trim can change the claim ("She had fillers done the conventional way…"). | A fresh agent answers problem / usual approach / used? / instead and why, from `comprehension_input.md` only. |
| U5 | When he contrasts two approaches, show both side by side and mark the one he doesn't use "Not used" (needles vs cannula; another syringe vs one). | `beats_v2.json` lists the contrast beats; review. |
| U6 | No blue highlight, tint, box or zone fill, and no flat fill over footage: markup belongs to the glass system (token colours, glass edge, shadow). Examples of a named condition are the only exception, inside a glass card, labelled "Illustration". | `tools/palette_check.py` (static colours and accent tokens; `--pixels` on the bare pass) and `lint_compositions.py` PAL / BOX1 / BOX2 = 0. |
| U7 | A before/after shows what was done and what changed, measured: entry points and cannula paths read on decoded frames and registered to her face, the change as a trough profile against a control line; arrows only above the noise floor. Detectors that disagree with the footage are logged and dropped, not tuned until they pass. | `sketch_measure.json` (entry error per side vs the 15 px target), `verify_v2.json sketch_accuracy.arrows.each_above_noise_floor`. |
| U8 | Drawn anatomy is traced from the model (face.glb), checked against a same-angle render, placed from her landmarks, and marked ostad_check: pending. | `anatomy_check.json`: bone outline difference median ≤ 3 px, p90 ≤ 6 px. |
| U9 | Read the critique before you render again, and fix each row with a check that proves it: a full-frame title card ≥ 120 px held 60 f, no blur fill anywhere, text-only beats replaced by objects (a syringe, not the word "lidocaine"), ≥ 3 topic-only animations from the model (the tear trough in section, needle vs cannula at a vessel, Tyndall light scatter). | `variety_check` R3 (text-only 0/13) / R5 / R8; `grep blur(` in the reel = 0 fills; per-row table in the report. |
