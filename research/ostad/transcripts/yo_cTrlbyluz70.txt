Learn on acne scars in under 60 seconds by a dermatologist. Let's go. Stop new pimples first. Every new breakout is another chance for scar. Not all acne scars are the same. Icepick, box car, rolling, and raised scars all need different treatments. There is no single best treatment for scars. Most need a combination. Subcision is the best for releasing tethered rolling scars. TCA cross works best for deep icepick scars. Microneedling or RF microneedling helps stimulate collagen and improve texture. Fractional CO2 laser can provide significant improvement, but comes with more downtime and isn't suitable for everyone. Fillers can instantly improve selected depressed scars. PRP alone won't erase scars. It may support healing, but is not a standalone treatment. No cream can completely remove established acne scars. Don't fall for miracle scar products. Aim for improvement, not perfection. Most patients achieve 50 to 80% improvement, not 100% scar-free skin. Treat acne early. Preventing scars is always easier than treating them.
