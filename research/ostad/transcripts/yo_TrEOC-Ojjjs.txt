If you have these deep scars in your skin, you might think you can just scrub them away. Please don't, because these aren't just dog spots or marks. They're actually acne scars called atrophic scars, meaning it's underneath your skin. This usually happens when you have a deep inflamed pimple and your skin doesn't make enough collagen while it heals. So when the pimple goes away, your skin underneath can sink in, leaving you with a dent in your skin. Now you don't want to treat the acne scars if you're still breaking out, because you're trying to fix the damage while your skin is still creating more. Instead, you want to get your acne under control first. And you can start with a topical retinoid, because this helps prevent new breakouts and post-acne marks. And when you're left with the deep scars, a dermatologist can help with precise treatments, such as chemical peels, subsision, or laser research.
