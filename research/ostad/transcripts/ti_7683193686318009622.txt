This is not a yeast infection, and it's not vitiligo either. This is progressive macular hypomelanosis. It appears as smooth like patches on the chest, back, or abdomen, and is often confused for pityriasis versicolor. But unlike pityriasis versicolor, it isn't caused by a yeast. Pigment loss is thought to be linked to Cutibacterium acnes disrupting melanin production. Treatment includes benzoyl peroxide, topical antibiotics, and for more persistent cases, phototherapy. So if you've been treating light patches with antifungal creams and shampoos and nothing is changing, you could have the wrong diagnosis.
