How can you tell a melanoma from some normal? But you want to look for the ABCDE. A is asymmetry. Does one side look different than the other? B is border. Is it nice and round? C is color. If there are multiple colors, that's something to be more concerned about. D is diameter. Is it about 6 mm or is it bigger than a pencil eraser? And E is evolution. Does it look different than it did 6 months ago? Check with your doctor.
