Rosacea is a condition where we see redness on the face, usually central facial redness, so around the nose, forehead, chin, and it can be accompanied with acne bumps as well. And there are considered to be four types of rosacea. There's the erythematotelangiectatic type, which is just straight up redness, and then there's the papulopustular rosacea, so the rosacea that gets a lot of acne bumps associated with it. There's ocular rosacea, so you can get rosacea in your eyes, and then there's something called rhinophyma, where people get like a buildup of sebaceous tissue in their nose. like pretty big noses. When it comes to just basic skin care with rosacea, the main thing is just keeping things gentle. Gentle cleansers, gentle moisturizers, gentle sunscreens. I recommend mineral-based sunscreens over chemical sunscreens. Brands that I really like that have gentle cleansing ingredients are CeraVe, La Roche-Posay, Avène, and Aveeno, as well as their moisturizers.
