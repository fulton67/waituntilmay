How is cosmetic mole removal performed? I'm a board-certified dermatologic surgeon. I've removed over 10,000 moles in my practice and these are the three main ways in which I remove moles for cosmetic purposes. Number one, laser mole removal. Now, this procedure is generally reserved for moles that are more superficial. So, smaller, lighter in color and flat. Now, a dermatologist needs to first verify that these moles are completely benign before they're able to be safely removed by laser. Number two, shave removal. Now, this procedure is generally reserved for moles that are bumpy because the surgical blade is used to cut across the base of the mole, flattening it out just like the rest of your skin. Now, it's important to remember that shave removal doesn't guarantee that the mole won't grow back because the base of the mole may still be present. Number three, surgical excision. Now, this is a little bit more complicated of a procedure and is generally reserved for moles where patients want it completely gone and out. So, surgical blade is used to cut out the entirety of the mole along with a little bit of normal skin and the sides of the skin are then stitched together which forms a small line scar. Now, the appearance of the scar will eventually fade with time so that the ultimate appearance of the scar is much less visible than the original mole itself. Have a conversation with their dermatologist to see which procedure is right for your mole. [Music]
