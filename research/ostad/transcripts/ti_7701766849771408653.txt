It's been over three months and you're still purging. That's not purging. In fact, I'd be willing to bet that for 99.999% of you guys it was never purging in the first place. Purging? It's real. It can happen, but it's not as common as the internet would have you believe. Purging refers to the temporary increase in the number of acne spots that you get when you start a topical that increases the rate of skin cell turnover. Typically that would be a retinoid, but in theory it can happen with other things, but in reality it's rare. Importantly, when it does happen, it does not go beyond three months. An increase in acne or continued acne beyond three months is not purging. It's either skin irritation that's worsening the acne, or your acne is not adequately controlled by topical retinoids.
