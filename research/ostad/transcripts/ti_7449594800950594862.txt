Things I don't care about as a dermatologist. I don't care if you splash your face with cold water, warm water, or filtered water. Your skin doesn't care either. Just wash with a gentle cleanser that works for you. I don't care if your skincare has activated charcoal or fairy dust. Detoxing your skin isn't real. Your liver and kidneys have that part covered. I don't care if your serum comes in a glass dropper or plastic pump unless it's vitamin C. For everything else, focus on the ingredients, not the packaging. I don't care if your sunscreen leaves a white cast for 30 seconds. Protection is non negotiable. Blend it and go on with your day. I don't care if you've been told that coconut oil is a natural miracle for your face. It clogs pores and wrecks your barrier. So keep it in the kitchen. I don't care if your cleanser says for all skin types or pH balanced. What matters is how your skin feels after. If it feels tight, it's not good for your skin. I don't care if your skincare products are made with ancient remedies or super foods. Trendy ingredients don't mean better skin. Stick with proven ingredients. I don't care if someone swears that beef tallow will heal your skin. It's outdated, heavy, and pore clogging. Modern moisturizers are better for your skin barrier and better for your breakouts. I don't care if your products are labeled for men or for women. Skin Is skin and these labels are just marketing. And the pink tax often times means you're paying more for the same formula and prettier packaging. Use what works for your skin type, not your gender. I don't care if your skincare says chemical free. Everything is made of chemicals, including water. What matters is that the ingredients are safe and effective, not the marketing buzz words. At the end of the day, I don't care about the trends, the marketing, or the fancy claims. What I care about is healthy, happy skin, and that comes from science, not hype.
