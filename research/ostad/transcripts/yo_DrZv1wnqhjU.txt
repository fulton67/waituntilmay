When you have rosacea, people are constantly asking you if you just got a sunburn. This is more than an hour after I went on a run. Granted, it was very hot. We're in Arizona. I had sunscreen, hat, everything. This is not a sunburn. This is just what rosacea looks like. This is the type 1 rosacea, which is flushing. You can imagine it's very embarrassing as a dermatologist to look like you have a sunburn after exercising in the heat.
