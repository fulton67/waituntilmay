What's the deal? Why is my tretinoin no longer working? Did my skin become resistant to it? This is something a lot of people end up encountering when it comes to using a topical retinoid. The benefits that they observed in the beginning, maybe they've observed them for a while. All of a sudden, it doesn't cut it in. Your acne, it's breaking out. So does this stop working? No. Topical retinoids do not stop working. Nor does your skin develop tolerance to or resistance against. Topical retinoids work by binding to certain receptors and skin cells and influencing the genes that are expressed, ultimately playing a role in how they act. And that doesn't change with ongoing use. What is more likely is that your skin condition is no longer well controlled by this topical alone. In the case of acne, this is not actually unusual. Acne is influenced by your hormones, which can change throughout your lifetime. It doesn't mean that this stop working. It just means that perhaps you need to see your dermatologist because maybe you need additional treatment in order to now exert control above and beyond a topical retinoid.
