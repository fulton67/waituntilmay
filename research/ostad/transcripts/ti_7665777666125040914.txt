I had skin cancer surgery two months ago. This is my second time having surgery on my face. But I guess they say that scars tell a story. I got my stitches out 10 days post up and the wounds healed so well. It's now been 2 months and it's time to start tackling the scarring. I do need to tell you something really funny, though. As soon as I woke up from the anaesthetic, the first thing I can remember is telling the nurses about my dog, ha ha. I wasn't able to wash my hair for a few days, so it definitely was not looking good. I've been putting silicone scar tape on my nose. There isn't much I can do about the scarring on my scalp, but I'm not too worried about that. When closing the wound on my nose, the skin seemed to have been pulled up and so I am fully convinced that my nostril is bigger and this is something that I have not gotten over yet. But anyway, that's just a bit of an update.
