Skin cancer 101 part 4. Not all skin cancer looks like an abnormal mole. Some skin cancers appear to be completely normal. Basal cell carcinoma can often appear as a shiny, pearly pink bump, and a lot of times they can actually look like normal pimples. But the thing that usually distinguishes A bcc from a pimple is that a basal cell carcinoma will hang around for months without ever fully healing. Another possible sign of skin cancer is a dry patch that won't go away no matter how much you moisturize. Or it can be a tiny delicate sore that bleeds just by washing your face. And melanoma isn't always brown or black. Some melanomas have very little pigment and can appear pink or skin coloured. We call these amelanotic melanomas, meaning they don't have the classic pigmented appearance. So rather than going based off of a few photos on what skin cancer should look like, focus on whether a spot is changing, growing, bleeding, crusting over, or just not healing.
