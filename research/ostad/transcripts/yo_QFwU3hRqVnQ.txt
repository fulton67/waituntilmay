When should you start retinol? If you're thinking about using retinol for anti-aging purposes, there's tons of information out there about how and why it works. But a common question that I get asked all the time is, when should I start? Here is the technical answer. Age 20. I know it sounds super young, but hear me out. Starting at the age of 20, your skin loses about 1% of its total collagen content every single year. And one of the main benefits of retinol is collagen production. So if you're considering retinol for anti-aging purposes, this is technically probably the earliest age that you can start. The reality is, people are using retinoids at a much younger age for conditions like acne and psoriasis. Don't get me wrong, if you're past your 20s, you can still do retinol and derive benefits. Even though the best time to start retinol was in your 20s, the second best time is now.
