I've always dreamed of having a seat at the table. As a patient and a provider, I've witnessed disparity within the aesthetics community. It's always been a challenge to envision myself as part of the industry and even harder to find a familiar face to look up to. The standard of beauty has been tailored to one race my entire life. Did you know, according to the Forces of Beauty report, that only 52% of women agree that anyone can be beautiful or black women's ideas of beauty were far less likely to have originated with society standards? Or that only 17% of all women, including Caucasian women, feel like their racial beauty is accepted by society? I believe it's time for change. The dream initiative is an action plan to drive much needed change across the aesthetics industry. Join us in our efforts to welcome all people, patients and providers alike to a seat at the table. It's time to make our dream a reality. To learn more about the dream initiative and the Forces of Beauty report, visit www.dreamforequity.com.
