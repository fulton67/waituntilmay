How I transformed my skin in my 30s as a dermatologist who has melasma and there is no filter here and no it is not lasers. Having gone through IVF this is my skin the day I gave birth. Hormones were a BITCH when it came to my melasma. Here's three things I did to take control of my skin. Number one prescription medications is the only way that you can treat your melasma but it comes with a few caveats because the number one gold standard to treat your melasma is hydroquinone. When using hydroquinone you need to cycle on it two to three months on, two to three months off and you've got to do this unfortunately in perpetuity because if you stay on it you can develop unwanted side effects, pigmentation that will never go away and you can even become resistant. Which leads me to number two over-the-counter skincare. Skincare can be a very powerful tool and that is why having a solid over-the-counter skincare routine in addition to the prescriptions on and off is key. Everything you use has to target how you produce pigment. Which leads me to number three. It is not a single ingredient story. Vitamin C alone, glycolic acid alone, tranexamic acid alone, retinol alone are never going to help you or your melasma. You have to marry this whole list of active ingredients that target pigmentation in your routine and that is why it is so key to find products that formulate these ingredients at effective levels together so you can minimize steps in your routine so you can actually overcome this bitch.
