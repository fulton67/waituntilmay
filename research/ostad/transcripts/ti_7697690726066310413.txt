Silicone sheets are used in burn units, but pressed onto a healing scar daily, they can help flatten and fade it. Centella asiatica is a trendy Korean skincare ingredient, but it has evidence for wounds healing and reducing inflammation. Bakuchiol is marketed as a natural retinol alternative, but it does not work through the same receptors, so it is not a true substitute if you need real retinoid benefit. Glycerin is one of the most boring ingredients on a label, but it remains one of the most effective humectants studied. Rice water has gone viral for skin brightening, but the evidence behind it is mostly anecdotal, unlike niacinamide or vitamin C. Oatmeal is a breakfast food, but colloidal oatmeal is an FDA recognized skin protectorant that calms eczema and itchy irritated skin.
