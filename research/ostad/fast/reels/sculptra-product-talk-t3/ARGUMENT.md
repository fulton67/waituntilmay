# Argument map (product-talk)

Title: **Sculptra vs hyaluronic acid**

- **The product**: Sculptra is polylactic acid, a powdered suture material injected under the skin.
- **What it does**: It triggers a tissue response that makes collagen, a type that can lead to scarring.
- **Compared**: Hyaluronic acid filler does not cause the nodules, inflammatory reactions and scarring seen with bio-stimulators like Sculptra.
- **Best for**: Hyaluronic acid used strategically suits people who want to sculpt the face with a long-term plan.
