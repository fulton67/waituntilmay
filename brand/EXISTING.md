## Type

Identified by eye; Phase 3 runs the crops through the font pipeline.
- **Display:** a high-contrast transitional/didone serif in roman and caps. It looks like the Cormorant / Bodoni Moda family. Wide-tracked caps carry small labels ("THE TRANSFORMATION", "ARIEL OSTAD MD").
- **Script/italic accent:** a calligraphic italic ("*Skincare*", "*Sclero Season*", "*online now*").
- **Sans:** a geometric/humanist sans (Montserrat-like) for event details and buttons ("JOIN NOW · LINK IN BIO").
- **Logo:** a thin open ring over "DR. ARIEL OSTAD / MEDICAL GRADE SKINCARE" (skincare line) and "Ariel Ostad MD" (practice).

## Five layout habits

1. **The transformation card:** a navy frame, "THE TRANSFORMATION ★★★★★" at the top, the before/after photos stacked or side by side with navy/sand BEFORE/AFTER tags, and a bottom bar with the procedure in serif on the left and "ARIEL OSTAD MD @drarielostad" on the right. 22 of 60 posts.
2. **The review card:** a navy or ivory ground, a centred serif quote, ★★★★★, the patient's first name and initial, and "VERIFIED PATIENT".
3. **The product poster:** a silver/ivory product photo with a stacked serif title, centred and editorial ("FALL *Skincare* EVENT", 15% OFF).
4. **The event card:** his portrait on navy, a big serif headline, a sans info block, a CTA bar.
5. **Text sits on its own ground**, not over the photo: separate bars and panels, with generous ivory margins. On the designed templates the photo takes about 70% of the frame and the bar about 15%.

## Tone of the captions

The median caption is 110 words, and 49 of 60 posts carry hashtags. Five tone words:
- **reassuring:** "surgery isn't always the first step";
- **educational:** "Niacinamide … has been extensively studied for its role";
- **measured:** "doesn't mean it's the right treatment for you";
- **grateful/personal:** "Always grateful for every patient…";
- **premium-clinical:** "elevated by thoughtful formulation".

Captions often open with a question or a gentle correction of a trend, then explain, then invite.

## What must not change

These are what make the account his and what his audience recognises:
- the navy + ivory pair;
- a serif voice for the headline;
- the transformation-card structure (the procedure named, then "ARIEL OSTAD MD");
- the ring mark;
- testimonials as a format.

## Where the board disagrees

The board is grotesk-led, image-over-type, frosted panels, skin full-bleed, oversized letterforms, and mono metadata. His grid is serif-led, with text on its own bars, centred symmetry and gold-luxury accents. Phase 4 reconciles them: keep navy/ivory and a serif voice; bring in the board's grotesk for structure, its mono for data, skin/face full-bleed and frosted panels; retire script italics and the generic luxury centring.
