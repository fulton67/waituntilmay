## How it was measured

Cuts: HSV histogram Bhattacharyya distance > 0.35 and > 3× the median of the surrounding second; shots shorter than 0.25 s merge. Transition length = consecutive frames with distance > 0.12. Camera per shot: Farneback flow at 160 px wide. Median magnitude < 0.12 px/frame = static; |radial| / magnitude > 0.55 = push or pull; consistency > 0.6 = pan or tilt; otherwise handheld/subject. Text: easyocr every 0.5 s at 540 px wide, confidence ≥ 0.4. A reveal counts the frames before the first sighting in which the difference inside the text box is more than 2× the difference outside it. All thresholds are judgment calls and are listed in `motion.json` → `thresholds`.

The flow classifier called the board reel "handheld/subject motion". The frame-level files overrule it: border drift says the camera is locked, and the similarity fit shows the zoom. The relabel is in `scripts/motion_report.py`, and the classifier's label is kept as `camera_flow_classifier`.

Limits: OCR events count string changes, so OCR jitter inflates the 3264 events; the shares are the usable numbers, not the count. The feed's 14% "tilt" is mostly two long shots of scrolling text pages (DdCI9zyuvgh, Dd4MSBHOic4). Rows of the board pattern do not alternate direction reliably: only 21% of adjacent bands move in opposite directions (`board_flow.json` `alternating_adjacent_band_share`), so no rule depends on that.

## Where this disagrees with `RULES.md` (BUILDER F)

- Its rule 2, "never add digital zoom or push; no scale keyframes on footage", cites the board camera as handheld. Measured, the board reel is a locked frame whose content zooms 1 → 4.47× → 1.01× (rules 2–4 here). For graphic or product motion, scale keyframes are the board's main move. The two can be reconciled by keeping "no digital zoom on filmed footage" and allowing a scale move on graphic or product layers.
- Its durations (enter 600 ms, exit 300 ms, dissolve 400 ms, hold 2.5 s) are judgment calls and can stay as judgment calls: the board has no text or transitions to time them against. Measured anchors that could replace or check them: 1.5 s zoom in, 2.6 s hold, 2.8 s zoom out, both moves ease-in-out.
- Its shot-min of 4000 ms is close to rule 1's 4.5 s (the feed p75).
