## Ostad video rules (YouTube v6 and its gap rounds, DECISIONS 14l–14p)

Hand-written from the measured ExoMind v6 (`research/ostad/style/exomind/gap/ROUND1.md`, `ROUND2.md`). Every Ostad edit follows them; the workflow is the `ostad-video` skill. The scale is 1920 × 1080 at 24 fps (for 1080 × 1920, keep the same px on the 1080 side).

| rule | number | check |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrows.** One system, `devices/Arrow.tsx`: brand blue `#37a9e8` on every arrow; stroke tapers 2 → 8 px; one filled barb head (`HEAD_K` 4.6); soft shadow + 1.4 W glow; draw-on tail → tip in 7 f (route trace 24 f), alive while held, retracts on exit. 9 variants (curved-callout, straight-pointer, bracket-pointer, route-trace, arc-connector, dashed-flow, light-ribbon, orbit-arc, marker-tick). Every arrow lands on a meaningful target. No dot-and-line callout. | connector ≥ 120 px; 0 text crossings (the first 12 px of the tail and the last 30 px before the tip exempt) | `lint_compositions.py`, `arrow_text_lint.py` |
| **Title and section cards.** A title card opens, a section card marks every turn of the script, an end card closes. The section card is one look for all four: number + rule + "Chapter", the title typed on under a travelling light, full frame over a real, moving picture with a left darkening (0.72 → 0.18). | type-in 12 f, **hold 60 f fully lit** (duration.hold-min 2500 ms), letters out 8 f: 80 f; title ≥ 120 px | a still at the hold midpoint (start + 42 f); pixel hold run = 60 f |
| **Immersion.** Images fill the frame and always move. Footage beside him sits in a clean rounded window or fills the frame; side-fill is the cream room, never a blurred copy. | mean image fill ≥ 35 % (v6 42.5 %); full-frame takeovers ≥ 3 /min (v6 4.65); no layout family for > 3 segments or 15 s; 0 blur side-fills; every image layer changes ≥ 0.16 grey levels over its life | `immersion_tools/measure_v6.py`, `variety_check.py` R13, `lint_compositions.py --static-image` |
| **Head tracking.** Anything that follows him is driven by `exomind/track.json` (MediaPipe Face Landmarker + One Euro) through `devices/track.ts`. Face-anchored tails sit on `templeR + (40, −6)`. The circular pip is a true `clip-path` circle with his face-box centre on its centre (head box 46 % of the diameter). Any straight edge of his source frame is feathered: the keyed plate's right 140 source px, the pip's left 16 %. | tail error on rendered pixels median < 6, p95 < 15, max ≤ 15 px (v6 B03 3.0 / 5.6 / 7.5, B16 2.2 / 4.9 / 9.7); pip offset < 4 % of the diameter (v6 1.9 %); straight-edge frames 0 (v6 61 → 0) | `exomind/gap/tracking_tools/gap_track.py`, `gap_pip.py` (on the render), `edge_check.py` |
| **No empty signifiers.** Every image is the literal subject of its line: his own products, his rooms, his patients and their own words, his device on a head in his office. No stock face with a zoom box, no ring pointing at nothing. Dates on screen match the real event. | beats with an empty or partial signifier: 0 (v6 4/20 → 0/20) | the beat sheet's `noun` + `assets` per beat; a stills sheet of every beat |
| **No reuse across topics.** A graphic made for one subject never appears under another; reuse the method, never the graphic. Footage an earlier edit showed full frame needs a new crop or role. | pHash + dHash distance to every earlier edit > 0.107 | `reuse_check.py` |
| **Same-subject cap.** One image subject on at most 2 beats. A diptych shows two different subjects, never two moments of one clip. | ≤ 2 beats per subject | `variety_check.py` R14 |
| **Licensed film marks.** A manufacturer film's own logo, emblem and burned-in subtitles are fully in or fully out of every frame and window; crop the band, never cut through a mark, never dissolve two marks over each other. | 0 frames with a mark cut by a frame or window edge | stills at every film beat |
| **Sound.** One sound per event class, from `beats.json sfx_map`: draw `exo_draw` (0.20 s, 7.2 kHz), card `exo_card` (0.32 s, 0.4 kHz), chapter `exo_chapter` (0.75 s, 2.9 kHz), reveal `swish`, chip `pop`, tick `yes`. Hero event of a beat 0.32, the rest 0.16. | SFX within ±2 f of ≥ 72 % of entries (v6 78.0 %); −14 LUFS integrated; true peak ≤ −1 dBTP (v6 −3.8) | `gap_audio/`, ffmpeg `ebur128=peak=true` |
