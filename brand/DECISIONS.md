21. **The scoring rule is a judgment call, written down before the result was read:** `board + favourite + 0.5 × instagram` per role, rank weights 1…1/5, favourites already ×3 on the board. Neue Haas Grotesk wins headlines (21.8 vs Poppins 13.0) and text (129 vs Helvetica Neue 37.8). So the system is one family: Display 65 Medium for headlines, Display 55 Roman for captions and labels (the crops matched Display 55 Roman about twice as often as Text 55 Roman). No mono passes the 10% bar.
22. **Losing systems are the best display face of each other Adobe classification** (from each family page's own filter tags): Poppins (sans) and Cormorant Garamond (serif). Their families were added so they render. On Instagram the account itself matches the serif route, and SYSTEM.md says so.
23. **The specimen renders through a web kit** (`font_create_kit`, kit vzz0hgt; domains localhost, 127.0.0.1, waituntilmay.com), because Creative Cloud's desktop activation doesn't expose the new families to headless Chromium. 24. **Tracking is calibrated, not copied.** pixels.json tracking includes each font's sidebearings, so every kit font was rendered at zero tracking and measured with pixel_metrics' own `glyphs()`. Letter-spacing is the difference (headline +0.008 em, caption −0.016 em, label +0.010 em). Story (1080×1920) sizes reuse the 4:5 sizes: a judgment call, since a story is read at the same width.
25. **The post grid comes from Instagram text placement** (`composition/grid.json`). Margin is 9.1% of width, the median left margin with the 27 of 59 posts whose text touches the edge left out (full-bleed type or UI, not a margin habit). The specimen's earlier 5.4% included them. The specimen now reads its margin and safe areas from the grid and was re-rendered (14 plates, kit fonts loaded).
26. **Shapes (Phase 4) are counted by presence:** images showing each class, favourites ×3 (`scripts/shapes.py`). Pill (4), circle (3) and grid (1) fall below.
27. **Shape thresholds are judgment calls**, stated in the script header: grain = high-pass std ≥ 2.5 grey levels in flat areas; circle circularity ≥ 0.85; blob cut-offs. The SVG stroke is floored at 0.15% of width, against the measured median of 0.139%, so a hairline survives export. The grain SVG's opacity and frequency are judgment calls scaled from the measured std of 2.17.

Each k=5 aggregate cluster sits a median CIEDE2000 of 3.25 (board) and 2.75 (Instagram) from its nearest k=6 cluster, and every chosen role is within 2.9 of a k=5 cluster, except the signal at 7.0 (`palette.json` k_check).

34. **Board devices: presence is tagged by eye, parameters are measured** (BUILDER Phase A). 35. **Board glass is matte, frosted glass, not glossy.** Measured on 41911984, 41432513 and 2145281 by fitting `inside = α·tint + (1−α)·blur_σ(backdrop)`. The backdrop is blurred by normalized convolution outside the card, and σ is the value that maximises R². Results: α 0.63 / 0.60 (band 0.44); σ 1.9% and 1.4% of the canvas (band ≥ 5%, with R² still rising at 64 px); rim light under 1.3 L*; no edge shadow. So the component has neither rim nor shadow. The two panel radii (1.6% and 3.9% of the canvas) differ, and the kit takes the median, 2.74 cqw. On 41432513 the blur is ill-conditioned, because the backdrop is already out-of-focus bokeh and R² is flat across σ.
36. **Glass tint takes the palette surface role, not the measured hue.** The board's glass picks up the hue of the photo behind it (#a9bea4 and #909a82 are green on green), so the kit uses surface #c3bdbb at the measured α. The dark card's ground is the palette text role #211b15, not the measured #000 / #261209. The pill fills map to background and surface.
37. **Components are sized in container units.** The post root `.ok` is `container-type: size`, so 1 cqw = 1% of canvas width. The same CSS then renders 1080×1350, 1080×1080 and 1080×1920 without per-size numbers. Pills and outline boxes are sized in em of the label role, so they track the type scale.
38. **The photo treatment ships as an SVG filter; the CSS shorthand is a fallback.** The best CSS chain (`saturate(.5) contrast(.7) brightness(1.075)`) sits a median ΔE 4.86 from the LUT across the feed sample, and 13 on a dark post, because it cannot lift blacks or shift the cast. `kit/components/treatment-filter.svg` carries the LUT's per-channel curves exactly (feComponentTransfer tables), plus a least-squares 4×5 colour matrix for chroma and cast. It sits ΔE 0.49 from the LUT on the sample and 1.07 rendered in Chromium. Chromium only resolves same-document filters, so templates inline it.
39. **Every device is proven in the browser.** Each one is rebuilt from `kit/components` at its source's canvas size and re-measured with the source's own estimator (`look/proof_check.json`). Line devices are drawn on an inpainted clean plate of the same photo. Matches: screen radius 58 vs 58 px; outline box stroke, text and padding 1 / 18 / 20×18 vs 1 / 18 / 19×16; pill outline stroke 4 vs 4 px; icon ring stroke 2 vs 2 px; step pitch 157 vs 157 px; glass radius 27 vs 27 px. Leader strokes are 1.25 vs 1.5–1.75 px FWHM. The dashed connector (0.3 / 0.28 cqw) and the ring numerals were matched by eye, because the source runs are too JPEG-soft to measure. Grain overlay opacity is calibrated to σ 0.0026: 0.06.

43. **Autofit steps down the type scale; it never goes off it.** A slot tries its role size, then the next roles down: headline → 0.8 → 0.64 of headline → caption → 0.88 of caption (`STEP` in `kit/templates/engine.js`). If a slot still overflows, it either paginates (caption-card text, carousel-body body; split at the longest word prefix that fits) or truncates at a word and returns a warning. The proof checks both cases (`kit/templates/templates_check.json`): truncate warns at 120 of 145 words, a long caption gives 2 pages, and a 7-slide carousel numbers 1/7..7/7.
44. **9:16 reuses the 1080×1350 type sizes inside the story safe area.** The safe area is 13.021cqh at the top and 17.708cqh at the bottom (DECISIONS 24). 1:1 uses the 1080×1080 sizes from fonts/specimen/spec.json. 46. **Font tokens are `--ok-display` and `--ok-font-text`.** The text font token was called `--ok-text` and overrode the palette `--ok-text` colour later in the same rule. 47. **Auto mode is rules, not a model** (`kit/templates/auto.js`, shared by the builder and post.py). Each caption is cleaned: NFKC, then URLs, hashtags and emoji are stripped. Every template whose rule matches becomes a candidate, in a fixed order: announcement (a date or time plus an event word), list (3+ bullets), before-after, quote, procedure-card (a named treatment), carousel (3+ paragraphs), single-statement (a hook of 14 words or fewer), story (9:16 only), caption-card (always). The first auto run takes candidate 0. Regenerate moves the seed on by one, which steps through the candidates, the accents and the photos. Text is fitted to each slot's `max` by whole sentences, then clauses, then a word cut. Across the 60 feed captions, seed 0 picks: carousel 21, procedure-card 16, before-after 10, announcement 7, list 3, quote 2, single-statement 1.
48. **The server PNG route runs locally, not on the deployment.** `/api/brand/ostad/render` drives `brand/scripts/render_posts.cjs` against the site's own `render.html`, so the route and post.py run identical code. It follows the precedent of `/api/render-real`: the Vercel deployment has no Chromium, so GET reports `available: false` and the builder disables its download buttons with a note. `OSTAD_RENDERER=on` enables it on a host that has a browser. Images in a spec must be data URLs or kit photos, so the headless browser never fetches arbitrary URLs.
