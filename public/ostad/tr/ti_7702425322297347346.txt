Why is it that when you're stressed, your skin condition starts to get worse? This is because there is a real connection between stress and skin. Stress can actually influence the inflammatory condition of your body, your immune function and the skin barrier itself. That's why people experience acne, redness and easy irritability when they have a lot of stress. But stress does more than just influencing your skin itself. It actually changes and influences a lot of the hormonal systems in your body, influencing your sleep and getting you to have cravings at night and changing the daily routines that you have already set. The a I skin analysis helps us objectively assess the visible changes in your skin, but also helps us monitor the changes over time. A biology body scan gives us additional information about the body's overall condition and gives us insight on building a plan for recovery.
