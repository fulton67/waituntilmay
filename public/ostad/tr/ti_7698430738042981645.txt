Like flowers, azelaic acid and tretinoin is a great combination, but a lot of people get the timing wrong. It's not about using them together, it's about splitting them in the same routine. One goes on in your morning routine and the other at night. And here is why. Apply your azelaic acid in the morning, then layer with your moisturizer and sunscreen. Azelaic acid is gentle enough for daytime use and won't necessarily make you more sensitive to the sun. At night is when your retinol or tretinoin comes in. Start with a pea size amount just a few times a week and follow with a moisturizer to buffer any irritation as your skin adjusts. So the bottom line is azelaic acid by day, tretinoin or retinoid by night, and sunscreen no matter what.
