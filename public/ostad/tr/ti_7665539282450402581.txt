From your mid-20s, intrinsic factors and environmental stresses contribute to the loss of collagen and elastin. This leads to fine lines wrinkles and loss of dermal support over time. You might also notice changes in hyperpigmentation, reveral skin tone and texture. A combination of non-invasive clinical treatments can help to slow this process down. Superficial chemical peels will accelerate epidermal turnover while preserving skin barrier integrity. Skin needling creates micro injuries within the epidermis and the dermis, initiating the wound healing cascade. This leads to collagen synthesis and improvement of things like fine lines wrinkles and overall loss of volume in the skin. Laser treatments can also be utilized. Our pico laser is great as well as our derma V. This will help with things like collagen production as well as overall tone and texture. And lastly, LED therapy uses non-thermal wavelengths to modulate cellular activity. This helps with collagen production as well as reducing inflammation within the skin. Pre-drivenation is about prevention, not correction. We want to make subtle changes that accumulate over time to protect the quality of the skin as well as delaying the need for more invasive, intensive procedures.
