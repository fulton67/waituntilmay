If you have blackheads on your nose, you see these and you think you might just scrape it off yourself. Don't. You're actually making them worse. See, when dead skin cells and debris get mixed with your skin's oil, they clump together and start building up inside the pore. When that build-up reaches the surface and gets exposed to air, you get these dark, raised blackheads. So, when you grab one of these to scrape those spots away, you're doing more harm than good. You actually risk more skin inflammation, larger pore appearance, and a higher chance of hyper pigmentation. Instead, use a topical retinoid. It helps regulate how cells lining your pore behave, reducing blackhead formation. And the most important part, give it time. After several weeks of consistent use, then you'll start seeing results.
