I discovered the only correct way to wash your hair because regular shampooing makes absolutely no sense to me anymore. I call this the Sandwich Method. Conditioner before shampoo might sound crazy, but this is a hack that I can get behind. I'm a dermatologist, I specialize in hair and if your scalp gets greasy while your ends are still dry, rough or damaged, then the hair sandwich method is one trend that I actually rate. It's basically when you apply conditioner, shampoo followed by conditioner. And the reason why it can work is because your scalp and your ends don't have the same need. Your scalp is where you're dealing with oil, sweat, dead skin cells, product build up. That is where shampoo actually needs to do its job. But your ends are actually the oldest part of your hair. They've had the most brushing, heat, colouring, which means that your cuticle can be more damaged and the hair can lose moisture more easily. This is exactly how you're gonna nail the sandwich hair method. So before you shampoo, take a small amount of conditioner through your mid lengths to ends. The first layer coats the hair fibre, gives you more slip, and helps reduce friction while washing so your dry ends aren't getting unnecessarily roughed up whilst you're trying to clean your scalp. Then shampoo the scalp properly. You do not need to aggressively scrub shampoo through your ends for a normal wash. The lather running through them When you rinse is more than enough. Then you're gonna finish with your conditioner again. To bring back softness, slip, and manageability after cleansing. This is especially useful if your hair is damaged. But if your hair is fine, that could weigh it down easily. Or you can have lots of build up so you won't need sandwich. Every wash, your scalp and your ends are allowed to need different things.
