One of the questions that comes up from patients in the exam room is what is the difference between a lower eyelid blepharoplasty versus using fillers. Lower eyelid blepharoplasty is really used to treat bulges under the skin of the lower eyelids. When people have excessive fat bulges that push forward, those are generally people who are good candidates for removing those fat pads. By making an incision under the lashes or approaching them from inside the eye. There are however many occasions where we can avoid the surgery and use under eye fillers. Those are usually people who have hollows in their lower lid creases. And by loss of collagen in that area, you get some small little what we call herniations which is protrusions of the bulges forward. By injecting a little bit of filler under the eyelids in those creases, we can provide support and therefore push back those fat pads inside and therefore make them less noticeable. So there are instances where eyelid fillers can be used. Eyelid fillers when used appropriately work really well. The key is to inject very small amounts. The results truly last for a couple of years. But yet there are instances where if someone has a severe amount of fat bulges, those have to be surgically removed.
