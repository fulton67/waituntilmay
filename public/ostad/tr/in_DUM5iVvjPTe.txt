Let's talk about this. Basin, coffee powder, milk, do they remove tan instantly? Short answer, no. Tanning happens because UV rays increase melanin production in your skin. Scrubs like coffee or basin only remove dead skin cells, so your skin may look temporarily brighter. In fact, harsh scrubbing can cause micro tears, irritation and more pigmentation, especially in Indian skin. Real tan reduction needs sun protection in clinic treatments like chemical peels, laser treatments and proper skincare, not kitchen hacks. If tan is bothering you, get it treated safely by a dermatologist.
