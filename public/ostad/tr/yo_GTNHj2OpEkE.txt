Dermatologist here, I'm gonna explain to you why overlined lips might look good in pictures but usually look crazy in person. In this photo here, obviously you see a significant texture difference between the vermilion lip here and the cutaneous lip up here. You can also see where they border each other. Usually in photo and video, we don't have depth perception like we do in person. So if there's a flash, if there's good lighting, if everything's blown out, you might lose some of the definition that gives away your overlined lip. However, if you're walking around in person, there's light, there's shadow, and people have binocular vision usually. So your actual lip contour is going to show through.
