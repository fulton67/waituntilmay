Acid and glycolic acid is truly a trio that is one of the best out there. But I'm a dermatologist and I'm telling you that not everyone actually needs all three of these. Azelaic acid is an anti inflammatory, so it's a great add on if you deal with underlying red, but if that's not you, you can go ahead and skip it because tretinoin alone can carry most of the work. And glycolic acid as an exfoliant once or twice a week is excellent, but if your skin tends to be sensitive, swap it for something like lactic or mandelic acid instead. You get the same exfoliating benefit, but they're much gentler. And the one rule that matters more than which ones you pick is to never stack all of these in the same routine.
