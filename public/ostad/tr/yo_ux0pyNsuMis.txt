Hi, I'm Dr. Soch, and I'm going to talk to you about the main symptoms of melanoma skin cancer. This is particularly important as we're approaching the summer months. Melanomas can appear anywhere on your body, but they're most common in sun-exposed areas. Make sure you check your skin regularly for any unusual changes. You can use a mirror or ask a friend or partner to check areas that you can't see. Melanomas often an uneven shape. They may have two different shaped halves and uneven edges. They can also be a mix of two or more colors, whereas normal moles are only one color. Whereas normal moles are usually small, melanomas tend to be bigger, often more than 6 mm wide. You should also be on the lookout for any moles that may change shape, size, or color, as these could be a sign of melanoma.
