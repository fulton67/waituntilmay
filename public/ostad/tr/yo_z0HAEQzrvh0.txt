Three skin treatments I prioritized in the past year that gave me the biggest confidence boost. 2026 has been my postpartum year and with the hormone drop and lack of sleep, my skin was looking tired, dull, pigmented, and these treatments really helped. Number one is microneedling. I really locked into this after having my baby. I did this every six to eight weeks. It basically creates micro injuries in the skin to stimulate a collagen response. This results in very plump skin. It helps to even skin tone, helps with texture. I love all types of microneedling, but I most often get this with PRF or exosomes to get an even stronger collagen response. Second is PRF and PRP EZGEL injections. I mostly do this for my under eyes. I could do a whole separate video on under eyes because this is what they used to look like with makeup on. With both treatments, your blood is taken, it's spun in a centrifuge to separate all the growth factors, the stem cells, all of that good stuff, and then re-injected back into your face. So in that sense, you could say it's more on the natural side. I've had two sessions of PRF and one session of PRP EZGEL, and from my understanding, the biggest difference is the consistency. PRF is a thinner, lighter consistency that can help to improve the overall skin quality. PRP EZGEL uses a process of heating and cooling to create a thicker biofiller-like substance, which gives a more immediate volume improvement. For me, it just completely wakened up and brightened up my entire face. Considering how I'm a new mom that often gets little to no sleep, I constantly get compliments on my under eyes. Third and most recent treatment is SkinVeve. Like a traditional filler, SkinVeve does involve injecting a hyaluronic acid substance into the skin, but unlike a traditional filler that is injected deeper into the skin, SkinVeve is injected superficially, so it targets overall skin quality and deep hydration. And because it's so amazing for hydration and plumping up the skin, I noticed that it also helped with any texture irregularities and any little fine lines, especially around my smile lines. I'm already obsessed with this treatment because one, it's given my skin a very smooth, almost filter-like appearance, and it's really boosted the whole radiance of my skin.
