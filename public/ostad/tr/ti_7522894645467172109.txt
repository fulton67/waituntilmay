As a skin cancer surgeon, I hear many myths about Sun Protection. One of the biggest myths is that everyone doesn't need sunscreen. Everyone, no matter how dark your skin is, you need sunscreen. Another myth that I get is that a base tan is good. You know, “if I get a little bit of a tan that, means I'm not going to burn. I'm actually protecting my skin”. This is false. There is no such thing as a safe tan. Any tan is sun damage. Any sun damage means that you are putting yourself at risk for skin cancer. Another myth is that you can only get skin cancer if you burn. Absolutely not. You know, skin cancer is caused by chronic U V exposure and I often liken skin cancer to the straw that broke the camel's back. It’s that one last exposure that's enough to turn your cells awry. And to give you enough DNA damage that now you have cancer.
