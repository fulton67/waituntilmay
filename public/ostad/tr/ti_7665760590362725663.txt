A boil doesn't suddenly appear. It starts when bacteria get trapped deep inside a hair follicle. Your immune system immediately attacks the bacteria. White blood cells flood the area. But as they die along with bacteria and damaged tissue, a thick pocket of pus begins to form beneath the skin. As more pus builds up, stretches the skin, causing pain and swelling. Eventually, the boil drains, releasing the trapped pus and allowing the infection to begin healing.
