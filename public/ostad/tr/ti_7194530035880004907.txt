Hi, I'm a dermatologist and here are three skincare mistakes that I see my patients make for those with brown skin or darker skin number one using high strength retinol or even vitamin C that could be causing tingling burning all that is too strong and could cause worsening of hyperpigmentation number two over exfoliating your skin either by using the physical scrubs that are too harsh or even for some folks with darker skin salicylic acid might be too strong. Instead, I recommend lactic acid, mandelic acid and number three DIY skincare such as putting lemon toothpaste on your face and some people that can cause serious skin irritation and again worsening of the hyperpigmentation
