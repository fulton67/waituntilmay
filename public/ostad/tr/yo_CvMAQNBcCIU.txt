Purple, white, gold, silver, all with PDRN. Today I'm comparing four Rejuran serums, breaking down which one is best for which skin type and concern. Rejuran Derma Healer Pore Tightening Ampoule. Like its name, this one really stands out for being focused on pores, excess oil, and uneven skin texture. It's a very lightweight, watery ampoule that absorbs quickly without feeling heavy on the skin. It contains 0.5% c-PDRN, 5% PDRN ex and witch hazel extract for anti-inflammatory and antioxidant support. This is the one I recommend to those of you with combination oily skin with concerns with visible pores, excess sebum, and textured skin. Rejuran Turnover Ampoule Dual Effect. This is where we're moving away from pore care and more focused on hydration, barrier support, and well-aging. Formulated with c-PDRN, exact percentage not specified, then ceramide NP to help strengthen and support the skin barrier, niacinamide, which supports the barrier while also helping with uneven skin tone, plus sodium hyaluronate and urea. I'd recommend this to dehydrated skin that's oily but dry at the same time, and combination mature skin with uneven skin tone wanting hydration, barrier support, elasticity support, and brightening all-in-one. Rejuran Moisture Treatment Ampoule. As the name suggests, this is an intensely moisturizing ampoule. This contains 7% PDRN and 0.5% c-PDRN. It also contains adenosine, which supports well-aging and helps with the appearance of fine lines, and sodium hyaluronate plus ceramide NP for hydration. I would choose this if you have very dry or dehydrated skin, or sensitive skin with compromised skin barrier needing hydration, soothing, and barrier support.
